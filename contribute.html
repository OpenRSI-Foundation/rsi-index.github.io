RSI-Anything

Challenge the frontier models with your own research.

Idea → ready task in ~1 hour Your baseline becomes the exam

RSI-Anything is the contribution pipeline for OpenRSI Index. It turns your research idea into a ready-to-run harbor task through an automated workflow. The agent asks for your input only when a task-defining choice genuinely needs it.

Five steps.

Create a proposal

Clone the pipeline repository and open a fresh Codex or Claude Code session in it, on a frontier model at its maximum reasoning setting. Trust the repository hooks when the client asks: they attest the session, and without them the trajectory cannot be submitted later. Then ask for the proposal-agent skill — one session, one proposal. It walks you through the research question, baseline, evaluation, scope, and compute budget, and drafts the proposal. Review it before you confirm.

Submit the proposal

Confirm, and the agent posts the proposal as a Task Ideas Discussion for you. Review is automatic and reruns on every edit. When feedback arrives, go back to the same session: the agent reads the Discussion, revises the proposal with you, and updates it in place.

Automatic task building

A PASS on the initial check starts the build on its own. The bot only interrupts for a genuine task-defining question, which you answer in the Discussion with /task. At ACCEPTED it goes on to generate a private task repository. Keep the original session open — you will need it once more.

Run it on your GPUs

The private repository is a complete workspace: the Harbor task, the read-only RSI-Harness, and the validator and runner skills. First ask the original session to upload the proposal trajectory and Discussion record. Then, on a machine with the GPUs your task declares, validate the environment, run the task through the harness, and upload the completed experiment trajectory.

Caution

We review every task for scientific soundness, rigor, and novelty, and independently reproduce it. Approval and trajectory submission do not guarantee inclusion: a task with an unreasonable, unsupported, or irreproducible design may still be excluded.

Share feedback

Leave brief feedback on proposal-agent and the pipeline in this Discussion. With the experiment trajectory pushed, your task is ready for review. Thank you for contributing to OpenRSI Index.

Scope of our RSI tasks

Anything related to a foundation model are in our scope. Vertical foundation model development like medical, legal, 3D vision, or scientific are also welcome.

A note on compute · Most contributions need GPU access

In most cases you will need the GPU resources your task calls for. Choose a task scope, experiment plan, and compute budget that fit what you have.

  1. This cycle asks for the trajectory as well as the task. Alongside the harbor task you design, you submit the agent's actual run on it, which means running the task on your own GPUs.
  2. Tasks that need no GPU are welcome too. Harness optimization is one example.
  3. A proposal-only window may open next cycle. Then no compute of your own is needed, and you will not have to run the trajectory yourself.
  4. No GPUs? You are still welcome to build with RSI-Anything. We review every proposal and task design that comes in. If your task matters a lot for your research, contact us: when it is a high priority for the benchmark, we will run the trajectory for you on our own compute.

Two ways in.

PATH A

Bring your coauthored representative project

Contribute a representative, fully open project you helped build. You know its open research directions, and its reward-hacking risks, better than anyone else.

PATH B

Start from a domain well-known project

Pick a domain influential open project that is closely aligned with your domain expertise, and design an RSI task around one of its experiments.
